# About the clinic

XYZ Dental Clinic has been caring for families in Koramangala, Bangalore since 2009. Our team has four dentists, two hygienists and a dedicated children's dentist.

# Address and directions

The clinic is at 42, 5th Cross, Koramangala 4th Block, Bangalore 560034, on the first floor above Canara Bank. The nearest landmark is Forum Mall, about 800 metres away.

# Parking

Free parking is available in the basement for all patients. Two-wheeler parking is next to the main gate.

# Timings

The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 7 PM and on Saturday from 10 AM to 2 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

# Emergencies

For dental emergencies such as severe pain, swelling or a knocked-out tooth, call our emergency line on 080 4567 8900, available 24 hours a day.

# Services

We offer general check-ups, teeth cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, dental implants, crowns and bridges, teeth whitening, braces and clear aligners, and wisdom tooth removal.

# Pricing

A consultation costs 500 rupees. Teeth cleaning costs 1,200 rupees. A tooth-coloured filling starts at 1,500 rupees. Root canal treatment costs between 6,000 and 9,000 rupees per tooth. Dental implants start at 25,000 rupees per implant. Teeth whitening costs 8,000 rupees per session.

# Braces and aligners

Metal braces cost 35,000 rupees for the full treatment. Clear aligners start at 1,20,000 rupees. Treatment usually takes 12 to 18 months.

# Children's dentistry

Our children's dentist, Dr. Kavya Rao, sees children from age 2. Fluoride treatment for children costs 800 rupees.

# Insurance and payment

We accept Star Health and HDFC Ergo cashless insurance. We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and UPI. Zero-cost EMI is available on treatments above 20,000 rupees.

# Appointments

Appointments can be booked by phone or on our website. Please arrive ten minutes early for your first visit and bring any previous X-rays. If you need to cancel, please tell us at least 24 hours before.

# Hygiene and safety

All instruments are sterilised in a Class B autoclave, and we use single-use disposables wherever possible.

# Languages

Our staff speak English, Kannada, Hindi and Tamil.

# Doctors

Dr. Arjun Mehta leads implants and oral surgery with 15 years of experience. Dr. Priya Nair specialises in root canal treatment. Dr. Sameer Khan handles braces and aligners.
