# About Spice Route

Spice Route is a South Indian and coastal seafood restaurant in Jayanagar, Bangalore, seating 90 guests indoors and 30 on the terrace.

# Opening hours

Lunch is served from 12 PM to 3:30 PM and dinner from 7 PM to 11 PM, every day. The kitchen takes last orders 30 minutes before closing.

# Reservations

Tables can be reserved for up to 12 guests. For groups larger than 12, please call the manager for a private dining booking. Reservations are held for 15 minutes after the booking time.

# Menu highlights

Our signature dishes are Mangalore fish curry, Kerala prawn roast, Chettinad chicken and appam with vegetable stew. We also serve a full vegetarian thali.

# Prices

The vegetarian thali costs 450 rupees. The average meal for two costs about 1,800 rupees. The Sunday seafood buffet costs 1,250 rupees per person.

# Dietary needs

Vegan and gluten-free options are marked on the menu. Please tell us about allergies when booking; our kitchen handles peanuts and shellfish.

# Children

High chairs are available, and there is a kids' menu for children under 10.

# Parking

Valet parking is available at dinner for 100 rupees. There is paid street parking nearby at lunch.

# Private dining and events

Our private dining room seats up to 24 guests with a minimum spend of 30,000 rupees. We host birthdays and office parties.

# Takeaway and delivery

Takeaway is available during opening hours. We deliver through Swiggy and Zomato within 6 kilometres.

# Payment

We accept cash, cards and UPI. A service charge is not added to the bill.

# Accessibility

The ground floor dining area and restroom are wheelchair accessible.

# Drinks

We do not serve alcohol. Our fresh juices, filter coffee and tender coconut drinks are popular.
