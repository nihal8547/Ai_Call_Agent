# About Sunrise Residency

Sunrise Residency is a 60-room business hotel in Indiranagar, Bangalore, 25 minutes from the city centre and 50 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.

# Rooms

Room: Standard; Price per night: 3,200 rupees; Max guests: 2
Room: Deluxe; Price per night: 4,500 rupees; Max guests: 2
Room: Suite; Price per night: 9,000 rupees; Max guests: 4
All rooms include free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a work desk and a 43-inch smart TV.

# Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 2 PM and check-out is until 11 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and cost 1,000 rupees each.

# Breakfast

A buffet breakfast is served from 7 AM to 10:30 AM in the Terrace Café and is included in all room rates.

# Airport transfers

Airport pickup and drop is available for 1,800 rupees each way. Please share your flight number when booking.

# Parking

Free valet parking is available for hotel guests.

# Facilities

The hotel has a rooftop swimming pool open from 6 AM to 9 PM, a 24-hour gym, and a business centre. The Terrace Café serves all-day dining until 11 PM.

# Meetings and events

Our conference hall seats up to 80 people and costs 25,000 rupees per day, including a projector and tea breaks.

# Policies

Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before arrival; after that, one night is charged. Pets are not allowed. Smoking is not permitted inside the hotel. Guests must show a government photo ID at check-in.

# Extra beds and children

Children under 6 stay free when sharing the parents' bed. An extra bed costs 1,200 rupees per night.

# Laundry

Same-day laundry is available if clothes are handed in before 10 AM.

# Payment

We accept cash, all major credit cards and UPI. Corporate guests can pay by company invoice with prior approval.
